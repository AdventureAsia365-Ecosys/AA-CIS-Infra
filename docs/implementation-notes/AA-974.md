## Verify seed for the live check (`db/scripts/aa974_b2b_verify_seed.py`)

Dev had no approved VND FX row (only BTN and EUR), so a VND quote answers 503 `unavailable`, and the AA-973 seed's two
2-seat departures (the first one sold by the AA-973 check) leave no seats for the extend, cancel and re-hold checks.

### Decisions

- **run-script, not a migration**: Dev-only synthetic data that must never replay in CI or another environment.
- **Builds on the AA-973 seed** instead of repeating it: same inactive maker / checker staff rows, same plan, same
  contract. Missing seed staff, plan (sellable, with components) or published seed contract in force →
  `SCRIPT refuse what=aa973_seed`, exit 13, nothing written.
- **VND row by the seed maker, approval by the seed checker** (`booking.policy_approvals`, entity `fx_rate`): bk-007's
  trigger refuses an approval by the row's creator, and real staff are never written as authors of synthetic data.
  25000 VND per USD, buffer 0 (a reader can check a quote by hand), `as_of` today UTC, source `SYNTHETIC S248 AA-974`
  (≤ 64 characters, the contract's `FxLock.source`).
- **A VND rate already in force from another source is used, not shadowed**; someone else's pending row for today
  (`(currency, as_of)` is unique) is never approved by the script: it reports `SCRIPT warn what=fx` and
  `verdict ready=false reasons=fx`.
- **Three 4-seat departures at +28 / +35 / +42 days** (next free 7-day offsets inside the seed contract otherwise),
  written by the seed maker so the AA-973 cleanup retires them too. Told apart from the AA-973 ones by capacity 4;
  only departures 14+ days ahead count (extend is not capped by the start, the fee tier is stable for the run). The
  plan's `updated_at` is touched (products change signal), as the AA-973 seed does.
- **Locks**: `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on the AA-973 seed's key and on its own, in that order, because both write the
  same plan's departures.
- **Readiness without writing**: bk-021 registered, partner of tenant `…c972`, approved `b2b` rule, the settings
  `b2b_quote_valid_hours` / `b2b_hold_hours` / `b2b_hold_extend_hours` / `b2b_rehold_limit_per_day`, the B2B
  cancellation policy in force (default and any plan policy, with tiers `min_days:fee_pct`), and per departure the fee %
  a cancel would cost today. bk-021 tables are read only when bk-021 is registered.

### Tradeoffs

- **No cleanup action**: `fx_rates` / `policy_approvals` are append-only by design and a quote keeps the rate it was
  priced at; deleting the row would rewrite that history. A later real VND row (newer approved `as_of`) supersedes it.
  VND is not a B2C selling currency, so the row prices nothing outside the B2B API.
